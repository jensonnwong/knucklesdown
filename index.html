HEAD-TO-HEAD ARM WRESTLING

Lay the phone flat between you. RIGHTY puts PULL under your right thumb, LEFTY under your left.
Blue plays from the bottom, Red from the top.
Goal: pin your opponent's hand onto your pad. Best of 3.
  • TAP PULL builds pulling power. Faster taps = more power, each tap costs a little endurance.
  • HOLD PULL locks your current power in and braces (opponent pulls 30% weaker vs you). Holding drains endurance — harder the more power you're locking.
  • UMPH is a big burst (11 endurance, short cooldown). It hits hardest as a SNAP or COUNTER, or when they're gassed, venting or letting go of a hold.
  • SNAP: Umph while you've been holding 1+ sec = 1.5× burst.
  • COUNTER: Umph within 0.3 sec of your opponent's Umph cancels most of theirs and costs you half.
  • Let go to recover endurance. Hit zero and you're GASSED for ~2 sec: weak pull, no Umph, no hold.
  • Leverage: the further your arm is bent back, the weaker your pull.
  • Pressing before GO! is a FOUL — your opponent gets a head start.
Story mode: beat 3 circuits of opponents. Each one has a gimmick, and their grip flashes right before they Umph. That's your cue to COUNTER. Lose all 3 hearts and you restart the circuit.
Keyboard: Blue = A (pull) / S (umph) · Red = L (pull) / K (umph)
